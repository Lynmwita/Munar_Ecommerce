# Munar Luxury E-Commerce — Modern Luxury Fashion WordPress & WooCommerce Platform

A bespoke, high-fashion luxury editorial e-commerce platform built for high-end fashion ateliers, contemporary apparel, haute couture bags, and accessories in Kenya & East Africa.

---

## 🏛️ Architecture & Features

- **Theme (`wp-content/themes/munar-luxury`)**:
  - High-fashion editorial aesthetic (Cormorant Garamond + Inter typography, Deep Onyx, Warm Alabaster Linen, Brushed Gold).
  - Swiper.js full-bleed lookbook sliders and story carousel.
  - AJAX Slide-Over Cart Drawer & Wishlist with free delivery progress meter.
  - Bespoke sizing selectors, live stock indicators, and "Complete the Look" styling pairings.
  - Responsive luxury navigation with mega-dropdowns for Collections, Apparel, Bags, and Occasions.
- **WooCommerce Integration**: Custom luxury template overrides for catalog, single product, cart, and checkout.
- **Safaricom Daraja M-Pesa Gateway**: Direct STK Push payment flow with instant webhook callback reconciliation.
- **Concierge & Care**: Floating VIP WhatsApp support gateway and AI fashion concierge advisor.
